Design notes

Why this page works the way it does. It opens by itself after 3 baskets, or you can open it now.

  • Visibility of system status. The needle, score, streak, and progress dots update on every action, so you always know what the game is doing.
  • Feedback. Every shot gets a result message, a sound-free animation, and a score change. Makes also trigger confetti.
  • User control and freedom. The game is optional. Scrolling and the top menu always work, and the notes open without scoring.
  • Recognition over recall. Language, skills, and hobbies are labeled buttons. Nothing needs to be remembered or guessed.
  • Goal gradient. Three dots show how close you are to unlocking the notes, which gives a reason to try again.
  • Visual hierarchy. The hoop is the one loud element. Everything else is calm so it stays the focus.
  • Accessibility. Keyboard play with Space, visible focus outlines, readable contrast, and reduced motion respected.
